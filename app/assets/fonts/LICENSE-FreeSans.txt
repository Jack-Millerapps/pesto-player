Fallback note: the plan asked for DejaVuSans.ttf, which was not available
offline on the build machine. To switch, drop DejaVuSans.ttf (Bitstream Vera /
DejaVu license, free to bundle) into this folder; text_cache.py prefers
DejaVuSans.ttf when present and falls back to FreeSansBold.ttf.
